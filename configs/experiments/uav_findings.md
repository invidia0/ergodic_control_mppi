# UAV deployment — findings for the paper

> **Historical tuning log.** Its interpretation is superseded by
> `cross_campaign_report.md`. The ESS diagnosis and memory-off failure survive; most
> bandwidth/curl rankings do not separate at 18 seeds, and the five UAV/ideal Fourier pairs
> are not directionally consistent. Keep this file for provenance only.

Everything measured while tuning `configs/uav_profile.yaml` for the ROS 2 / SO3 deployment.
Written 2026-08-01. `results/` is gitignored, so this file is the durable record; the CSVs
it cites are regenerated by the commands given.

Environment for every number below unless stated: map seed 518, `map_fill` 0.002, start
(-15.57, 0.42), inflation 1.14 m, target density means (0, 6) / (-12, -4) / (12, -4),
offline ideal controller on the recorded grid, GPU, `--xla_gpu_autotune_level=0`.

## Headline

The shipped configuration reached all three target modes on **2 of 18 seeds**. It now
reaches them on **18 of 18**, with lower occupancy error. Two inherited defaults were at
fault, both of which looked deliberate, and neither visible in occupancy MSE.

**The two are not independent, and the order of the story matters.** `lam_max` was the
deeper fault; the lengthscale was partly compensating for it.

| arm | all 3 modes, `lam_max: 20` | all 3 modes, `lam_max: 1000` | occupancy MSE | in_mode | cycles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| h = 0.94 (old) | 9/18 | **17/18** | 4.505e-07 | 0.720 | 1 |
| h = 5.0 (shipped) | 16/18 | **18/18** | **3.866e-07** | 0.391 | 2 |
| h = 6.6 | 13/18 | 18/18 | 4.740e-07 | 0.388 | 2 |

Once the temperature loop works, the bad lengthscale largely recovers on visitation alone.
h = 5.0 is still the right choice — 14% better occupancy error, and `in_mode` 0.39 against
0.72 with 2 cycles against 1, i.e. genuine visit-dwell-exit-repeat rather than parking in
one basin — but **do not quote "2/18 → 16/18" for the lengthscale**: that was measured in
the argmin regime and overstates its contribution to a config we no longer ship.

Occupancy MSE stays within 3.8–4.7e-07 across every arm above. **Mode visitation and
`in_mode_fraction` were the only metrics that exposed either fault.** Any future tuning
scored on coverage error alone will miss this class of failure.

## 1. The memory lengthscale sets mode visitation

`configs/experiments/campaign_findings.md` recommends `fine_bandwidth: sqrt(h_f * h_c)`
= 0.94. That rule was fitted at modes ±6 and does not transfer to ±12.

At `memory_scales: 1` the bank is `geomspace(fine, coarse, 1)` = `[fine]`, so
`fine_bandwidth` is the sole active scale. The repulsion kernel peaks at `sqrt(h/2)`, and
that peak must land at **0.55–0.80 of a mode radius** (2.35–2.65 m here):

| h | peak | fraction of mode radius | all 3 modes (20 000 steps, 18 seeds) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.94 (campaign rule) | 0.69 m | 0.28 | 9/18 |
| 5.0 (**shipped**) | 1.58 m | 0.64 | **16/18** |
| 6.0 | 1.73 m | 0.70 | 15/18 |
| 6.6 | 1.82 m | 0.74 | 13/18 |
| 8.5 | 2.06 m | 0.84 | 0/6 (8 000 steps) |
| 11.0 (derived h_c) | 2.35 m | 0.95 | 1/6 (8 000 steps) |

Fails at both ends for opposite reasons. Too narrow and the memory senses only track
spacing, so nothing reports that a *mode* is saturated — the vehicle covers one basin well
and never leaves (median x_max 2.8 m, against modes at x = ±12). Too broad and the
repulsion spans the whole mode, evicting the vehicle before a dwell qualifies as a visit
(dwell falls to 7.9 s).

Rule for transfer: **`h ~ r_mode^2`**, i.e. place the single scale at the tuned coarse
bandwidth (~0.6 x derived `h_c`), not at the geometric midpoint.

### The scale bank dilutes rather than adds

`stein.py` averages the bank as `total / Q`, so extra scales split weight instead of adding
resolution. Q=3 over `[0.08, 0.73, 6.6]` spends two thirds of the memory on track-spacing
detail and reaches all three modes on 1 seed in 6 — as bad as having no coarse scale.
Q=2 and Q=3 with the top pushed to 11.0 recover only to 3/6. **No bank beat one
well-placed scale.** This confirms campaign finding 2 by a different route.

### Spatial and temporal scale trade off — do not tune independently

Once h is basin-sized the memory saturates a basin quickly, so a long `memory_time` makes
the whole workspace read as covered, flattens the excess field, and removes the gradient
that drives transits. At h = 6.6, tau = 8 reached all three modes on **0 of 6** seeds
against 5/6 at tau = 5.5. `memory_gain` behaves the same way: 8 → 4/6, 15 → 5/6, 30 → 2/6,
60 → **0/6**. With the lengthscale right the flow needs *less* push, not more — so the
campaign's `k_M = 60` must not be copied to a wide density.

At the shipped h = 5.0 the existing `memory_time: 5.5` and `memory_gain: 15.0` are already
best (9/18 against 6/18 at tau=3, 8/18 at gain=8, 4/18 at both). `memory_balance` is inert
to harmful (0.5 → 2/6, 1.0 → 1/6 against 0.85 → 5/6), confirming campaign finding 3.

## 2. The MPPI weights were one-hot: `lam_max` capped the ESS controller

Diagnosed from the rollout replay (`ergodic_control_mppi/mppi/replay.py`). On the flown
profile the temperature pinned at `lam_max = 20` from step ~250 onward and achieved
**ESS = 0.6% against an `ess_target` of 30%** — the update was argmin over 250 rollouts,
not a weighted average.

Regenerate: `python -m ergodic_control_mppi.experiments.uav_diagnostics ess --run-dir <run>`
→ `results/archive/2026-08-05/csv/diagnostic_ess.csv`.

| cap | converged lambda | ESS settled | pinned |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 (old) | 20.0 | 0.6% | yes |
| 1e3 / 1e4 / 1e5 | **151.8** (all three) | **33.9%** | no |

The loop is not broken and needs no replacement by a fixed lambda — it was capped an order
of magnitude below where it wanted to sit. It **self-limits**: caps of 1e3, 1e4 and 1e5 all
converge to the same 151.8, so the value is not sensitive and anything above ~200 works.

Outcome at 18 seeds x 20 000 steps: all three modes on **18/18** against 14/18, occupancy
MSE **3.866e-07** against 4.220e-07 (-8.4%). Caveat: **Fourier ergodicity is 14% worse**
(0.0506 vs 0.0443), breaching the ">10% worse" guard in the arm-selection rule.
`in_mode_fraction` falls 0.496 → 0.391: the vehicle parks less and transits more, matching
the spatial target better and its spectrum worse. The two metrics genuinely disagree here.

### Rescaling the penalty weights is *not* the fix here

The cost terms are badly out of scale — measured per rollout
(`uav_diagnostics costs` → `results/archive/2026-08-05/csv/diagnostic_costs.csv`):

| term | cross-rollout spread | per-step magnitude |
|---|---|---|
| grid/obstacle (`weight` 1e3) | 0 – 1.5e5 | 1000 per step in a cell |
| out-of-map (`oom_cost` 1e3) | 0 – 1.1e5 | 1000 per violated step |
| boundary margin (`boundary_weight` 100) | 0 – 7.5e4 | up to 225 |
| **flow + control (the objective)** | **1.5e3 – 5.3e3** | **1.5** |

One out-of-map timestep costs as much as ~660 steps of coverage optimization, so the
softmax ranks rollouts ~95% on constraint violation. That imbalance is real and worth
recording. **But it was not the binding constraint**: with the cap left at 20, scaling the
penalties by 0.1 or 0.01 still pins lambda and still gives ESS < 1%. Raising the cap alone
fixes it; rescaling as well lands ESS but no better (24–46%). Prediction made before
measuring — that lambda would need 500–900 and that only a combined arm could work — was
wrong on both counts.

## 3. Process findings

### XLA GPU autotuning breaks cross-process determinism

XLA:GPU selects GEMM kernels by timing candidates **at compile time**, so a loaded machine
picks different kernels and returns different float32 results. Bit-identical *within* a
process, which is why it hides. In this closed loop it amplified until identical-config,
identical-seed runs differed by **16 m of travel** and by whether all three modes were
reached; it manufactured a spurious "memory_gain = 60 wins" result that survived a 2-seed
check and died at 6 seeds. `ergodic_control_mppi/__init__` now pins
`--xla_gpu_autotune_level=0` (~6% runtime). https://openxla.org/xla/determinism

Consequences: any GPU comparison across separate processes before this carries an
unquantified error term, **including campaign cells**. CPU and GPU are each deterministic
but disagree with each other, so `device` is part of a run's identity.

### Six seeds cannot rank arms; run length confounds visitation

A 6-seed screen put h=6.6 at 5/6 and h=6.0 at 2/6; at 18 seeds they are 13/18 and 15/18 —
the ordering reversed. Treat any n<=6 ranking as a filter, never a result.

Separately, median time-to-all-modes is ~187 s. **An 8 000-step run is 160 s**, so it
truncates the tour and reports capability failures that are really clock failures: h=5.0
scores 9/18 at 8 000 steps and 16/18 at 20 000. Score visitation at >= 20 000 steps.

## 4. Deployment measurements

- **Only 2 of 10 map seeds (511–520) arm** at 1.29 m inflation: 514 and 518. Free space
  does not predict it — 516 is the emptiest map in the sweep and still fails. What decides
  it is whether a high-noise voxel lands within the inflation radius of a mode centre.
- **`OccupancyGrid.info.resolution` is float32.** Checking arming at float64 while the
  controller indexes at float32 disagreed by one cell and armed a map whose mode 2 was
  buried in an obstacle. Every run before that fix flew an unreachable mode.
- **`tracking_allowance` calibrated 0.20 → 0.05 m** from measured `pos_p95_m` = 0.013–0.024,
  taking inflation 1.29 → 1.14 m and free space 80.8% → 84.3%.
- **Inflation is not what limits coverage.** Flown clearance to the raw map: min 1.41 m,
  median 2.79 m, with only 0.71% of samples within 0.15 m of the inflated boundary. The
  vehicle never pressed the fence; it simply never travelled to mode 2.
- **The inflation budget mixes two kinds of term.** 0.61 m is pure geometry and has no
  reason to scale with speed; only the 0.53 m stopping distance does, as `v^2/(2a)`, and a
  static grid must apply it isotropically. Splitting the planning grid from a geometry-only
  collision grid would decouple them, but not before `brake_accel` is measured: with
  geometry-only inflation the non-body margin is 0.31 m against 0.53 m of stopping distance.

### Flown result (20 000 steps, `lam_max: 20` — predates the temperature fix)

collisions **0**, all three modes at 220.8 s, `mode_cycles` **1**, `step_p99` **9.07 ms**
(deadline 16), `deadline_miss_fraction` 5.0e-5, `achieved_rate` 49.79 Hz, `real_time_factor`
0.9998, `pos_p95` 0.0133 m, `max_speed` 1.70 m/s, `guard_fraction` 0.0136.

`guard_fraction` exceeded the <1% shortlist rule at `lam_max: 20`; **accepted as-is by
decision, do not retune to hit it.** It then fell to 0.22-0.28% on its own once the
temperature was fixed, on all five paired seeds.

### Paired UAV vs ideal, 5 seeds, fixed profile (`results/uav/paired_4*`)

| run | UAV MSE | ideal MSE | delta | UAV cycles | ideal cycles | all 3 modes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| paired_43 | 4.401e-07 | 4.434e-07 | **-0.7%** | 0 | 1 | no |
| paired_44 | 3.971e-07 | 3.576e-07 | +11.0% | 0 | 1 | 281.6 s |
| paired_45 | 5.393e-07 | 3.352e-07 | +60.9% | 1 | 0 | 266.0 s |
| paired_46 | 4.508e-07 | 3.358e-07 | +34.2% | 0 | 1 | 237.3 s |
| paired_47 | 3.682e-07 | 5.810e-07 | **-36.6%** | 0 | 0 | no |

Median +11.0%, range [-36.6%, +60.9%]. **Two of five seeds beat their own ideal twin**,
which is the signature of a variance-dominated difference rather than systematic
degradation.

Against the stated acceptance rule ("<=10% degradation *or* inside the ideal seed IQR"):

| metric | UAV median | ideal median | ideal IQR | gap | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| occupancy_mse | 4.401e-07 | 3.576e-07 | 1.076e-07 | 8.25e-08 | **PASS** (inside IQR) |
| fourier_ergodic | 7.304e-02 | 3.589e-02 | 1.915e-02 | 3.72e-02 | **FAIL** (+103%) |

Occupancy passes on the IQR form. **Fourier does not, and this one is not noise** — the UAV
is 2x worse and the gap is nearly double the ideal IQR, in the same direction on every
individual flight.

Why the two metrics disagree is informative rather than contradictory. The spectral metric
weights mode `k` by `Lambda_k = (1 + ||k||^2)^(-3/2)`, so k=(1,0) counts 0.354 against
k=(10,10) at 3.5e-4 — a factor of 1000. **It is therefore dominated by the lowest spatial
frequencies, and cannot be measuring tracking jitter.** What it is measuring is coarse
imbalance: too much time in one region of the workspace. `occupancy_mse` spreads that same
diffuse large-scale error over many small histogram cells where squared error stays small,
which is why it passes while Fourier fails.

That makes the Fourier failure the *same phenomenon* as the online visitation gap below,
seen through a different instrument, rather than an independent problem. Do not report the
deployment as matching its ideal twin spectrally.

### The online loop is NOT repeatable at fixed seed

Two flights, identical config, identical seed 43 (`paper01` and `paired_43`):
occupancy MSE **7.289e-07 vs 4.401e-07 — 65.6% apart.** ROS timing jitter alone. This is the
noise floor for any single online run, and it is larger than most effects being screened.

**Consequences: never rank two configurations on single flights.** Screen offline, where the
loop is deterministic, and use flights to verify safety, timing, and that the offline result
survives contact with the vehicle. Two n=1 online comparisons in this session's history were
retracted for exactly this reason.

### Online reaches all three modes less reliably than offline

Offline the shipped profile reaches all three modes on 18/18 seeds; online, on **3 of 5**,
and it takes longer when it does (237-282 s against a 169 s offline median). The gap is
real and unexplained. Candidates: the observation overwrite feeding executed rather than
predicted positions into the memory, the 50 Hz command rate against the offline
integration, and guard interventions perturbing the flow tracking. Worth measuring before
the deployment is claimed to reproduce the offline behaviour.

## 5. Scope decisions

- **2D planning at fixed altitude is deliberate**, to be justified in the experimental
  section by pillar-style obstacles, which span the full height and so make Z freedom
  unnecessary. 3D rollouts with a 2D density are degenerate unless altitude costs
  something: with z-independent coverage the optimal policy is to climb above everything
  and cover unobstructed, deleting the obstacle problem. The altitude-dependent
  (camera-footprint) coverage kernel that would fix that is covered in the literature and
  out of scope. Future marginal work.
## 6. The curl cannot be removed

Measured in the fixed-temperature regime, 18 seeds x 20 000 steps:

| theta | all 3 modes | t(all modes) | occupancy MSE | Fourier | cycles | in_mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0 (no curl)** | **13/18** | 174.7 s | **3.776e-07** | 0.0567 | 2 | 0.525 |
| 15 | 18/18 | **143.5 s** | 4.284e-07 | **0.0367** | **3** | 0.505 |
| 30 (shipped) | 18/18 | 169.1 s | 3.866e-07 | 0.0506 | 2 | 0.391 |
| 45 | 18/18 | 204.8 s | 6.079e-07 | 0.0552 | 1 | 0.375 |

**theta = 0 posts the best occupancy MSE of any arm tested and still loses 5 of 18 seeds'
mode visitation.** The campaign's hypothesis that the curl might be deleted outright does
not transfer to this density. It was formed on the reference environment where `no_curl`
won on ergodicity and error — precisely the metrics that cannot see this failure. Keep
`stein.theta`, and do not remove it from `stein.py`.

theta = 15 is a defensible alternative rather than a loser: fastest tour (143.5 s), best
Fourier by 27%, and the only arm reaching 3 cycles, at the cost of 11% worse occupancy MSE.
If the paper wants cycling rather than coverage error as the headline, 15 is the better
number. Shipped stays at 30 because occupancy MSE is the primary metric.

theta = 45 is strictly worse on everything (57% worse MSE, slowest tour, fewest cycles).
